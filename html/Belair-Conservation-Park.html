Cutting techniques

In all sections, the pollarded trees are to be repolladed when the branches are big enough, new trees that have grown to approx. 20cm diameter can be pollarded. Please be sure to pollard branches flush to the stump.

Cutting techniques

Follow on from what has been done in the past, continue to prune the pollarded trees. For those shooting from the ground please cut flush back to stump.

Seasonal Influences

No entry on extreme heat days
